# SEO Architecture

SEO for StrongAvatar.com must be controlled, registry-driven, and reference-led. It must not become random page generation, keyword stuffing, or mass-produced low-value publishing.

## SEO Doctrine

Search visibility should support authority around avatar strength, AI avatars, synthetic likeness, digital identity trust, digital humans, avatar governance, avatar ownership, avatar consent, avatar disclosure, brand avatars, creator avatars, and enterprise avatar readiness.

The purpose of SEO is to help qualified readers find governed reference material, not to inflate traffic with weak pages.

## Controlled Topic Architecture

Approved future SEO clusters may include:

- Avatar strength
- AI avatars
- Synthetic likeness
- Digital identity trust
- Digital humans
- Avatar governance
- Avatar ownership
- Avatar consent
- Avatar disclosure
- Brand avatars
- Creator avatars
- Enterprise avatar readiness

## Indexing Requirements

Every indexable page must have registry approval, strategic purpose, SEO cluster, buyer logic role, canonical URL, title, description, internal links, source status, and quality status.

No page is indexable by default.

## Rejected SEO Patterns

- Random SEO pages.
- Keyword stuffing.
- Mass-generated low-value pages.
- Thin glossary pages.
- Duplicate-intent articles.
- Generic AI avatar generator pages.
- Affiliate-driven ranking pages.
- Unsourced legal, compliance, technical, or market claims.
- Pages created before the quality gate exists.

## Current Status

No public SEO surface is active in Sprint 3. Sitemap generation and public indexing remain disabled.
