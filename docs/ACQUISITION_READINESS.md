# Acquisition Readiness

Acquisition readiness is a strategic discipline, not a sales page.

StrongAvatar.com should become easier to evaluate because its doctrine, registries, publishing controls, SEO architecture, monetization rules, and non-distressed posture are documented before public construction.

## Readiness Logic

Documentation helps a future strategic buyer understand what the asset is, what it refuses to become, how it controls public publishing, and how it can support a larger identity, trust, synthetic media, avatar, or digital human platform.

## Readiness Inputs

- Doctrine: defines the thesis, category, exclusions, language posture, and Avatar Strength Protocol.
- Registry discipline: shows that pages, navigation, sources, redirects, monetization products, and indexing status are controlled.
- Reference authority: requires source-backed, non-thin, internally linked, quality-gated content before public publishing.
- SEO architecture: supports controlled discoverability rather than mass page generation.
- Monetization architecture: permits only revenue models that preserve authority, trust, buyer logic, and acquisition readiness.
- Publishing controls: require validation before output and deployment.
- Non-distressed posture: avoids urgency signals, traffic inflation, and low-quality monetization.

## Strategic Buyer Relevance

The asset may become relevant to AI avatar and AI video platforms, enterprise creative and identity platforms, digital human infrastructure companies, synthetic likeness legal and compliance companies, gaming and spatial computing identity platforms, creator economy platforms, and deepfake protection or rights-management companies.

## Diligence Standard

A future buyer should be able to inspect the repository and understand the asset's strategic category, control model, publishing discipline, prohibited directions, monetization limits, and path toward reference authority.

## Current Status

The repository remains pre-public-site. No valuation claim, traffic claim, revenue claim, deployed website claim, or acquisition offer language is made.
