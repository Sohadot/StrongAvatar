# Monetization Architecture

Monetization is not implemented in this foundation phase.

## Architecture Principle

Future monetization must reinforce trust, reference authority, and acquisition readiness. It must not convert StrongAvatar.com into an affiliate shell or low-trust lead capture site.

## Future Eligible Surfaces

- Governance briefs
- Institutional reports
- Assessment tools
- Protocol-aligned advisory products
- Carefully reviewed sponsorships

## Current Constraint

No scripts, affiliate links, product pages, payment flows, ads, or tracking monetization are authorized.
