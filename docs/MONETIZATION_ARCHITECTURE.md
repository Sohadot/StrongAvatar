# Monetization Architecture

Monetization is not implemented in Sprint 3.

Future revenue architecture may exist only if it preserves authority, trust, buyer logic, and acquisition readiness. Monetization must never make StrongAvatar.com look like a niche affiliate site, low-quality avatar maker, ad surface, or mass SEO operation.

## Future Revenue Surfaces

- Paid strategic briefs.
- Manual Avatar Strength Audit.
- Controlled intelligence reports.
- Curated directory after authority exists.
- Newsletter as distribution layer, not the core asset.
- Sponsored listings only under disclosure and editorial control.

## Monetization Controls

- Revenue products must be registry-defined before publication.
- Sponsored or paid visibility must be disclosed.
- Paid placement must never override editorial standards.
- Monetized pages must pass content, SEO, source, internal-link, buyer logic, and quality-gate checks.
- Monetization may not create thin tool pages, random affiliate pages, popups, early AdSense surfaces, low-quality sponsorships, or undisclosed placements.

## Relationship To Acquisition

Controlled monetization can demonstrate product direction and buyer logic. Uncontrolled monetization weakens diligence, trust, and category authority.

## Current Constraint

No scripts, affiliate links, product pages, payment flows, ads, tracking monetization, sponsored listings, or monetized public pages are authorized.
