# Homepage Wireframe

The homepage is not implemented in Sprint 3. This document defines future structure only and does not authorize HTML, CSS, templates, public copy, JavaScript, images, components, generated output, or publishing.

## Strategic Role

The future homepage must position StrongAvatar.com as the reference layer for avatar strength and governed digital identity. It must not say "Create your AI avatar" or imply that the site is an avatar generator.

## Future Homepage Structure

1. Hero: StrongAvatar.com / The Avatar Strength Protocol.
2. Foundational line: A weak avatar is generated. A strong avatar is governed.
3. Category thesis: avatar strength, synthetic likeness governance, and digital identity trust.
4. Why avatar strength matters: explain the shift from generated media to governed representation.
5. Protocol overview: introduce authority, consent, context, integrity, disclosure, accountability, and institutional fitness.
6. Digital likeness governance layer: define the risk boundary around synthetic likeness, representation, misuse, and trust.
7. Reference system sections: guide users to future protocol dimensions, glossary, comparisons, briefs, reports, tools, and methodology after approval.
8. Who this is for: creators, brands, platforms, compliance teams, and digital human builders.
9. Future monetization surface: briefs, audits, reports, and a controlled directory after authority exists.
10. Strategic acquisition signal: non-distressed, institutional, and category-aware without valuation claims.
11. Footer: doctrine, methodology, quality gate, source policy, trust links, and controlled navigation.

## Content Constraints

- No generic avatar-maker promise.
- No prompt-gallery positioning.
- No thin SEO blocks.
- No affiliate-first calls to action.
- No unsupported claims about law, compliance, safety, or market adoption.
- No Arabic-language public positioning.

## Current Status

The homepage remains a wireframe concept. Public publishing remains disabled.
