# Design System

The design system is intentionally not implemented in this phase.

## Design Doctrine

Future visual design must communicate institutional trust, category clarity, restraint, and authority. It must not look like a prompt marketplace, avatar toy, AI novelty page, crypto landing page, or affiliate review site.

## Future Direction

- Editorial clarity over visual noise.
- Dense trust signals over ornamental effects.
- Protocol language over marketing hype.
- Accessible layouts over conversion gimmicks.
- Long-term authority over short-term novelty.

## Current Constraint

No CSS, visual tokens, component styling, or brand assets are authorized in the foundation phase.
