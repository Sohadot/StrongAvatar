# Source And Claim Discipline

StrongAvatar.com must distinguish conceptual analysis from factual claims before any public content is drafted, indexed, monetized, or acquisition-facing.

## Conceptual Analysis

Conceptual analysis may be original. The Avatar Strength Protocol, sovereign asset posture, and governed representation framing may synthesize original ideas without pretending that every concept is externally sourced fact.

Conceptual framing must be labeled and written as analysis. It must not masquerade as sourced fact, legal certainty, regulatory guidance, market evidence, company behavior, or platform capability.

## Factual Claims

Factual, legal, regulatory, technical, market, comparative, platform, and company claims must be source-governed before public publication.

No public content may contain unsourced factual claims once public publishing is enabled.

## Legal And Regulatory Claims

Legal and regulatory pages must include a reference-only / not legal advice disclaimer.

Legal and regulatory claims require source support from appropriate source classes such as regulatory sources, legal analysis, platform documentation, or company primary sources where relevant.

## Market And Acquisition Claims

Market-size, growth, valuation, traffic, pricing, buyer, and acquisition claims require explicit sourcing or must be framed clearly as strategic analysis.

Acquisition claims must not make valuation promises. Any buyer logic must remain strategic posture unless supported by explicit evidence.

## Company And Platform Claims

Company claims must rely on company primary sources or trusted platform documentation where possible.

Platform capability claims must be tied to platform documentation, technical documentation, company primary sources, or other suitable high-trust references.

## Comparative Claims

Comparative claims must be balanced, source-aware, and specific about the basis of comparison. StrongAvatar.com must not publish vague superiority claims, unsupported rankings, or affiliate-style comparisons.

## Source Quality

Source quality matters more than source quantity.

StrongAvatar.com must not use weak blog citations as primary authority for high-stakes legal, regulatory, technical, market, company, platform, or acquisition claims.

## Publication Rule

Before public publication, claim type, source requirement, disclaimer requirement, and citation status must be validated. If a claim cannot be sourced or clearly framed as analysis, it must not be published.
