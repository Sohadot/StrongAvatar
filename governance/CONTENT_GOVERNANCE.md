# Content Governance

StrongAvatar.com content must serve reference authority, not volume.

## Content Standard

Every public content item must advance the asset's position around avatar strength, synthetic likeness governance, digital identity trust, or governed digital representation.

Content must be tied to the Avatar Strength Protocol or to a controlled adjacent category: Digital Humans, AI Interfaces, Spatial Computing, Creator Identity, Brand Representation, Virtual Personas, Synthetic Media Trust, or Likeness Governance.

## Market Language Rule

StrongAvatar.com is a global English-language asset. Arabic is only an owner-operator working language and must not appear in public-facing content, SEO strategy, buyer-facing positioning, or asset doctrine except where this internal distinction must be documented.

## Publication Requirements

- Registered in the appropriate data registry before drafting.
- Assigned a strategic purpose.
- Mapped to a controlled topic cluster.
- Internally linked with intent.
- SEO-controlled before publication.
- Sourced when making factual, technical, legal, comparative, or market claims.
- Substantive enough to satisfy the future quality gate.
- Aligned with buyer logic or reference authority.
- Reviewed against `QUALITY_GATE.md`.

## Prohibited Content Behavior

Do not publish placeholder pages, generic AI avatar explainers, low-quality avatar maker content, unsourced legal assertions, thin keyword pages, scraped comparisons, fandom content, Arabic-language public content, trend-chasing avatar design posts, or content designed primarily for affiliate capture.

## Current Status

Public publishing is disabled. Content governance defines the future standard; it does not authorize public pages.
