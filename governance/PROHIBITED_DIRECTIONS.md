# Prohibited Directions

StrongAvatar.com must not drift into categories that weaken its strategic value.

## Prohibited

- Generic avatar generator positioning.
- Public AI image prompt galleries.
- Affiliate-first software roundups.
- Placeholder public pages.
- Thin SEO landing pages.
- Unsourced legal or compliance claims.
- Decorative-only design systems.
- Monetization scripts without approval.
- Public pages outside the registry.
- Arabic public doctrine, SEO pages, or buyer-facing content without explicit approval.
- Content that frames the asset as a blog, hobby site, parked-domain filler, or trend commentary feed.

## Enforcement

Any proposed work that enters a prohibited direction must be rejected or rewritten before it reaches public output.
