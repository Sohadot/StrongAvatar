# Buyer Logic

StrongAvatar.com should be legible to strategic buyers without being built only for sale.

## Buyer Thesis

The asset may appeal to organizations that need credible category language around trusted avatars, likeness governance, synthetic representation, enterprise identity, brand safety, or AI-mediated digital presence.

## Buyer Signals To Build

- Clear doctrine and category ownership.
- Structured registries and clean architecture.
- Original protocol language.
- Controlled SEO footprint.
- Evidence of quality discipline.
- Monetization paths that preserve trust.

## Buyer Risks To Avoid

- Thin traffic that cannot survive diligence.
- Public claims without sources.
- Overbuilt design with weak doctrine.
- Affiliate dependence.
- Confusing the asset with an avatar generator.
- Mixing public English positioning with unrelated internal-language artifacts.
