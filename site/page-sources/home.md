# StrongAvatar.com

StrongAvatar.com is the reference layer for avatar governance — the classification framework, the standard, and the assessment protocol that determine whether a deployed synthetic avatar or digital likeness is governed, accountable, and trustworthy.

This is not an avatar generator. It is not an aesthetic judge. It is the governance authority for the category.

---

## The Category Problem

Synthetic avatars and digital likenesses are deployed across platforms, products, and commercial contexts without a shared standard for what governance means. Consent is assumed or absent. Provenance is undocumented. Synthetic origin is undisclosed. Rights chains are ambiguous. Attribution is unbound.

The result is a category without a floor. Platforms cannot audit what they deploy. Regulators cannot evaluate what they govern. Rights holders cannot enforce what they cannot trace.

StrongAvatar.com defines the floor.

---

## The Framework

Three documents form the complete governance framework:

**[Avatar Integrity Ontology](/ontology/)** — classifies ten weakness classes (W-01 through W-10) that represent observable governance failures in avatar deployments, and eight governance components (C-01 through C-08) that remediate them. The ontology is the classification foundation from which everything else is derived.

**[Strong Avatar Standard](/standard/)** — defines four strength bands — Ungoverned, Provisional, Governed, Sovereign — that translate weakness class activations into a governance verdict any practitioner can apply without the author present.

**[Avatar Strength Assessment Protocol](/protocol/)** — defines the bounded questionnaire, the deterministic rule table, and the output specification for assessing any deployed avatar's governance band. Same inputs always produce the same band, the same dominant weakness class, and the same remediating components.

---

## The Ten Weakness Classes

The Avatar Integrity Ontology defines ten observable governance failures that this framework classifies and remediates:

- [W-01 Consent Absence](/weakness/consent-absence/) — no documented authority for likeness use
- [W-02 Provenance Void](/weakness/provenance-void/) — no traceable chain from source to deployment
- [W-03 Disclosure Failure](/weakness/disclosure-failure/) — synthetic nature not communicated where material
- [W-04 Identity Drift](/weakness/identity-drift/) — avatar operates outside its authorized scope
- [W-05 Impersonation Exposure](/weakness/impersonation-exposure/) — avatar can be cloned for impersonation
- [W-06 Rights Ambiguity](/weakness/rights-ambiguity/) — chain of title unclear or contested
- [W-07 Revocation Gap](/weakness/revocation-gap/) — no mechanism to decommission the avatar
- [W-08 Context Collapse](/weakness/context-collapse/) — avatar reused in unauthorized contexts
- [W-09 Attribution Loss](/weakness/attribution-loss/) — no accountable owner bound to the deployment
- [W-10 Robustness Weakness](/weakness/robustness-weakness/) — governance infrastructure is adversarially vulnerable
