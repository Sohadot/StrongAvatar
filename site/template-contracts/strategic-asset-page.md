# Template Contract

## Template Name

Strategic Asset Page

## Template File Planned

`strategic-asset-page.html`

## Construction State

Contract-only, no HTML template exists yet, non-public, non-generative.

## Purpose

Govern future strategic asset pages that explain asset posture without becoming sales pages or valuation claims.

## Allowed Page Types

Strategic asset page, acquisition readiness references, and internal-to-public posture pages after approval.

## Required Data Inputs

Route, title, strategic purpose, buyer logic role, acquisition posture, source status, prohibited claims, required links, SEO cluster, and quality status.

## Required SEO Elements

Title, meta description, canonical, robots, Open Graph tags, Twitter tags, and structured data when applicable.

## Required Trust Elements

Source note when factual claims exist, reference-only disclaimer where legal/regulatory content appears, last-reviewed marker, and methodology link where relevant.

## Required Internal Link Elements

Breadcrumbs, protocol link, methodology link, source discipline link, and related acquisition-readiness links.

## Required Buyer Logic Elements

May surface buyer logic as strategic context without solicitation, urgency, valuation promises, or sales pressure.

## Monetization Boundary

No monetization module may appear unless approved by the monetization registry and policy.

## Prohibited Template Behavior

Must reject generic blog layout, avatar generator landing page layout, affiliate directory layout, fandom page layout, thin SEO landing page layout, hype-heavy startup landing page layout, and unsourced claim presentation.

## Accessibility and Performance Requirements

Require semantic structure, readable hierarchy, lightweight output, no unnecessary scripts, and no visual clutter.

## Indexing Conditions

Templates do not control indexability; indexability must come only from registry approval and quality gate.

## Notes

This template must not make valuation, traffic, revenue, or buyer certainty claims without source approval.
