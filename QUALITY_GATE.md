# Quality Gate

The quality gate protects StrongAvatar.com from premature publication, weak SEO expansion, doctrinal drift, and low-trust monetization.

## Mandatory Publication Rule

No public page may be published unless it is registered in the site data registry, strategically purposeful, internally linked, SEO-controlled, sourced where factual claims require support, non-thin, non-placeholder, aligned with buyer logic or reference authority, and validated through the future quality gate.

## Required Checks

- The page exists in `site/data/pages.json`.
- The page has a defined strategic purpose.
- The page supports the Avatar Strength Protocol or a directly adjacent governance topic.
- The page strengthens reference authority or buyer logic.
- The page is internally linked with intent.
- SEO metadata is controlled, non-duplicative, and consistent with the registry.
- Factual, legal, technical, comparative, and market claims are sourced.
- The page is not thin, generic, placeholder, scraped, or trend-chasing.
- The page contains no unauthorized monetization.
- The page does not present StrongAvatar.com as a generic AI avatar generator, low-quality avatar maker, generic AI blog, affiliate shell, mass SEO page factory, fandom site, Arabic-language content site, or trend-chasing avatar design blog.

## Rejection Triggers

- Missing registry entry.
- No strategic purpose.
- Unsupported claims.
- Weak internal-link logic.
- Duplicate SEO intent.
- Public content before approval.
- Monetization pressure before authority exists.
- Any attempt to create page volume without reference value.

## Approval Outcome

A page may be held, revised, rejected, or approved for controlled indexing. Approval must be recorded before publication.

Current status: public publishing remains disabled.
