# Quality Gate

The quality gate protects StrongAvatar.com from premature publication, weak SEO expansion, and doctrinal drift.

## Mandatory Gate

No public page may be published unless it is registered, purposeful, sourced where needed, internally linked, SEO-controlled, and quality-gated.

## Required Checks

- The page exists in `site/data/pages.json`.
- The page has a defined strategic purpose.
- The page supports the Avatar Strength Protocol or a directly adjacent governance topic.
- Claims are sourced when factual, legal, technical, market-based, or comparative.
- Internal links reinforce the controlled architecture.
- SEO metadata is intentional and non-duplicative.
- The page has no placeholder content.
- The page has no unauthorized monetization.
- The page does not present StrongAvatar.com as a generic website, blog, avatar generator, or affiliate shell.

## Approval Outcome

A page may be held, revised, rejected, or approved for controlled indexing. Approval must be recorded before publication.
