C-06: Rights Chain Documentation

Definition

A documented chain of title establishing who holds rights over the likeness or identity, what those rights permit, and how they have been assigned or licensed to the deploying party.

Why This Matters

Rights chain documentation is the legal layer of governance that makes all other governance claims defensible. A well-documented consent basis, clear provenance, and visible disclosure are each only as strong as the underlying rights chain. If the deploying party does not hold rights over the likeness, no amount of documentation can make the deployment legitimate. Rights documentation is particularly important for commercial deployment — where licensing, assignment, and sublicensing questions arise — and for any deployment involving real persons' likenesses.

Remediates

  • W-06 Rights Ambiguity (primary)

Implementation Indicators

A deployment has implemented C-06: Rights Chain Documentation when:

  • Rights documentation covers the likeness source (real or synthetic) and all assignments
  • Licensing terms match the deployment context and are not expired or contested
  • The chain is traceable to an originating rights holder

Full Implementation Requirements

Full implementation requires E1-Y in the assessment protocol: rights documentation covers all assignments, matches the deployment context, and is current. The chain must trace from the originating rights holder through each assignment to the deploying party. Expired or contested documentation at any link in the chain means the requirement is not fully satisfied.

Integration with Other Components

C-06 integrates with C-01 Consent Infrastructure — rights chain documentation establishes who is authorized to give consent, while consent infrastructure records that consent. C-06 also integrates with C-07 Revocation and Kill-Switch Mechanism — rights clarity is a precondition for knowing who holds withdrawal authority.

Band Contribution

C-06 implemented deactivates W-06. W-06 deactivated is required for Sovereign classification. Governed classification allows W-06 partial.
